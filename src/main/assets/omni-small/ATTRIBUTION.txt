The bundled NCNN conversion preserves the original float32 weights. This
application's Vulkan image pipeline works in BGR plane order, so the conversion
adds one 1x1 permutation convolution in front of the network and one behind it
(R<->B swap); both are numerically exact. The model's trailing torch.cat of four
48-channel tensors is rewritten as a sum of four 1x1 convolutions (numerically
equivalent, and avoids depending on pnnx's Concat handling).

This 2x anime SPAN model fills the "Omni Small" model pack: the OmniSR
(2xHFA2kOmniSR) route was abandoned because pnnx crashes on that graph and its
dynamic interpolate gets baked to the traced size (see
.workbuddy/enhance-packs/README.md, section 5.2).
